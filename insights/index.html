Insights

Research notes and small experiments from our AI work

Short write-ups of experiments on AI agents, local models, AI in public services and open data on real-world problems. Each one links to the code and data, so the results can be checked and reproduced.

Clinical AI · 30 October 2026

In 20 clinic consultations, local AI lost red flags and the injured side when patients spoke Roman Urdu

llama3.1:8b lost 16 of 168 facts from Roman Urdu against 1 from English (p = 0.0003), and critical facts only in Roman Urdu. Both models almost never wrote left or right.

 Agentic AI · 30 October 2026

ProvenanceFlow: can tracking data origins secure AI agent tool chains?

A synthetic benchmark of 1,500 scenarios compares five policies. Field-level provenance preserved task completion while blocking simulated attacks, but only when metadata labels were correct.

 Medical imaging · 29 October 2026

I rebuilt my chest X-ray model: 0.80 AUROC on a patient-wise split, and probabilities up to 11 times too high

DenseNet121 reached a test mean AUROC of 0.801 on NIH ChestX-ray14 with the official patient-wise split. Its raw probabilities were 2.5 to 10.9 times the real prevalence until a per-class correction.

  • Calibration
  • Deep learning
 Clinical AI · 28 October 2026

Translating Roman Urdu first did not make a local AI's clinic notes safer

llama3.1:8b and qwen2.5:3b drafted physio notes from Roman Urdu, directly and after translating. Translating first raised missed critical facts from 3 to 5 and swapped left and right; the 3B model did not translate at all.

 Agentic AI · 29 October 2026

Late or lost revocation notices, and AI agents that try another channel

A notice one step late let 16.5% of revoked actions through in a simulator. After email was denied, qwen2.5:3b tried fax, post or a portal in 8 of 16 episodes; llama3.1:8b stopped every time with a rule.

  • Local LLMs
 Agentic AI · 29 October 2026

AI memory filters break when the labels are wrong, and no model said CONFLICT

With 20% of labels wrong, unsafe memory got past a gate in about 21% of questions. Given notes that disagree, neither qwen2.5:3b nor llama3.1:8b ever answered CONFLICT.

AgentRetry: when a tool times out, should an AI agent retry or verify first?

A reproducible synthetic benchmark compares four recovery policies and measures task completion, safe completion, duplicate-side-effect risk and escalation.

 Agentic AI · 26 October 2026

Small local AI agents followed hidden instructions in clinic notes. Checking the tool's arguments stopped them.

qwen2.5:3b and llama3.1:8b planned tool calls for 10 invented clinic tasks in English and Roman Urdu. A hidden instruction was followed in 6 to 8 of 10; only a rule that checks the patient and recipient stopped every one.

 Agentic AI · 25 October 2026

When AI agents' logs went missing together, every audit rule either missed gaps or raised false alarms

Three simulated agents lose their logs one field at a time or two at once. Judging only complete logs missed about half the skipped duties, and counting missing logs as failures flagged more than half of the clean episodes when outages were frequent.

  • Multi-agent systems
  • Synthetic benchmark

Three local AI models mixed up left and right in Roman Urdu, and were sure they were right

qwen2.5:3b and llama3.1:8b read the same physio notes in English and Roman Urdu. In Roman Urdu all three agents said yes to the wrong side on 3 of 4 questions, almost always with 100% confidence.

 Agentic AI · 24 October 2026

When AI agents fail together, more votes do not help

Three simulated agents that can fail for the same reason: majority vote accuracy fell from 79.9% to 70.0% as shared failures became more likely, and never beat the best single agent.

 Agentic AI · 24 October 2026

A one-number fix for overconfident agents worked, but barely

Subtracting the measured overconfidence improved the Brier score of three simulated agents at every level, but by less than 0.001, and calibration error did not improve.

 Agentic AI · 24 October 2026

Averaging agents' confidence made the team less trustworthy

Five ways to combine three simulated agents: averaging their confidence gave the worst calibrated team, and adding evidence in log-odds the best, once each agent was corrected.

 Agentic AI · 22 October 2026

A planted memory fooled a simple AI agent 74% of the time. A trust filter cut it to 9%.

A synthetic simulator of agent memory compares three retrieval rules. A trust filter removed most planted memories at no cost; a corroboration rule scored 0%, but only because of how the test was built.

  • Agent memory
  • Security
