About Masmatix

Engineering software since 2017

Masmatix Innovations is a software development, AI and digital marketing company with offices in Glasgow and Faisalabad.

Masmatix started in Faisalabad in 2017 with a simple belief: businesses deserve software built around the way they actually work. Since then our studio has delivered more than 700 projects, including business systems from supermarket checkouts and diagnostic lab software to weighbridge systems for flour and textile mills, many of them in use every day.

In 2025 we incorporated Masmatix Innovations Ltd in Scotland to work directly with clients across the UK. Our Glasgow office leads consulting, AI research and project delivery, and our Faisalabad studio designs, builds and supports the software.

What sets us apart is depth on both sides of the work: practical business software shaped by years on the shop floor, and research-grade machine learning backed by published, open-source work. Clients get one team for strategy, design, engineering and marketing, and a partner that stays with them after launch.
